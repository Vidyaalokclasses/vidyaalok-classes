About Vidyaalok Classes & Lead Faculty

Alokesh Nanda
Physics Faculty (JEE/NEET)

Vidyaalok Classes ("The Light of Knowledge") provides rigorous, concept-driven coaching for Foundation programs (Classes 8–10), Board Exams (Classes 11–12), and competitive entrance exams including JEE Main, JEE Advanced, and NEET.

The institute is guided by Alokesh Nanda, Physics Integrated Faculty at Narayana International School with over 8 years of specialized experience in JEE/NEET mentoring. An alumnus of Ramakrishna Mission Institutions (Midnapore & Rahara) and Hyderabad Central University (M.Sc. Physics), he holds a B.Ed. degree and was awarded the prestigious DST-INSPIRE Higher Education Scholarship by the Govt. of India after clearing both JEE and WBJEE.

Having served as an EdTech Project Leader and Content Strategist developing advanced problem-solving modules for top platforms like Vedantu, Unacademy, and Doubtnut, he brings cutting-edge pedagogy and structured problem-solving to Vidyaalok Classes.

🎓 Elite Pedagogy Rooted in Ramakrishna Mission values and HCU academic rigor.
🏆 Proven Track Record DST-INSPIRE Scholar and JEE/WBJEE ranker with 8+ years experience.
💻 EdTech Curriculum Former EdTech Content Leader for national learning platforms.
